Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Konekton (“the app”) is a free board and todo list editor at https://huntermac.github.io/konekton/. It is a personal project made by one independent developer (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

Short version

  • The app has no server and no database of its own. I do not collect, see, store, sell or share your data.
  • Your boards and files are stored only in your own browser and, if you sign in, in your own Google Drive.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Where your files are stored

  • In your browser. The app keeps a working copy of your boards and files in your browser’s local storage (IndexedDB, localStorage and sessionStorage). This data stays on your device. Clearing your browser data deletes it.
  • In your Google Drive (optional). If you sign in with Google, the app saves your files in a folder named konekton in your own Google Drive. The files go directly from your browser to Google. They never pass through any server of mine.

Cookies and browser storage

The app sets no cookies of its own. It stores in your browser only what is strictly necessary for the features you use, and nothing for tracking, analytics or advertising:

  • your boards and files (IndexedDB), so you can edit them and they survive a reload;
  • the Google sign-in token (sessionStorage), so you stay signed in while the tab is open;
  • the email address of the last Google account and an internal account key (localStorage), so the right files are shown after sign-in;
  • view settings such as the chosen view, zoom and grid (sessionStorage and localStorage), and short one-time notes shown after sign-in (sessionStorage).

Because this storage is strictly necessary for a service you asked for, no consent banner is shown. Google may set its own cookies when you choose to sign in with Google; see the Google cookie policy.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for these permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: create files and folders in your Google Drive, and see, edit and delete only the files and folders that the app created. The app cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Your basic account identity (an account ID and your email address), read from Google Drive. The app uses it only to keep the files of different Google accounts apart in your browser and to show which account is signed in.

How the app uses this data:

  • The app uses Google user data only to save, load and sync your own Konekton files between your browser and your Google Drive.
  • The sign-in token is kept in your browser’s session storage and ends after about one hour. Your email address is kept in your browser’s local storage so that the next sign-in can suggest the same account. Nothing is sent to me or to any third party.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train AI or machine learning models.
  • No person reads your data. I have no access to it.

Konekton’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app’s files. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when your browser loads the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google provides the sign-in and Google Drive. Your use of them is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing your data and access

  • Sign out in the app (the Sign out button in the top right corner) to forget the sign-in in this browser.
  • Remove the app’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the konekton folder in your Google Drive to delete the files stored there.
  • Clear this site’s data in your browser settings to delete the local copy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes

I may update this policy. The new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
